# Spinach Patties
> Category: Baby Food - Fiber

## 1. Ingredients
- Blanched spinach: 1 bunch
- Olive oil: some
- Nutritional yeast: 1/2 tbsp
- Egg: 1
- Whole wheat flour: 1 tbsp
- Milk: 1 tbsp

## 2. Steps
- Saute blanched spinach in olive oil and nutritional yeast.
- Dice up spinach mixture in food processor.
- Mix with egg, whole wheat flour, and milk.
- Cook on fry pan in olive oil in a thin sheet.
- Flip when the batter is mostly solidified.
