# The 4th Floor Falafel
> Category: Baby Food - Fiber

## 1. Ingredients
- WF Mediterranean blend: 1 bag
- WF Caribbean protein blend: 1 bag
- Olive oil: some
- Nutritional yeast: 1 tbsp
- Egg: 3
- Whole wheat flour: 3 tbsp
- Milk: 3 tbsp

## 2. Steps
- Saute vegetables in olive oil and nutritional yeast.
- Dice up sauteed vegetables in food processor.
- Mix with egg, whole wheat flour, and milk.
- Cook on fry pan in olive oil in a thin sheet.
- Flip when the batter is mostly solidified.
