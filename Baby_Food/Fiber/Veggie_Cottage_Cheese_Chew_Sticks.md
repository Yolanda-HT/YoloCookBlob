# Veggie Cottage Cheese Chew Sticks
> Category: Baby Food - Fiber <br>
> Yield: 24 sticks

## 1. Ingredients
- Broccoli slaw (or other veggies): 8 oz
- Low fat cottage cheese: 1/2 cup
- Nutritional yeast: 2 tbsp
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Egg: 1
- Whole wheat flour: 1/4 cup

## 2. Steps
- Mix ingredients in food processor.
- Spray lady finger baking pan with oil.
- Pipe mixture into baking pan.
- Bake at 350F for 25 minutes.
