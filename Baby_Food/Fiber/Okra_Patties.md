# Okra Patties
> Category: Baby Food - Fiber

## 1. Ingredients
- Steamed okra: 1 lb
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Nutritional yeast: 1 tbsp
- Mushroom powder: 1 tsp
- Egg: 2
- Whole wheat flour: 3 tbsp
- Milk: 1/4 cup

## 2. Steps
- Saute steamed okra in olive oil, nutritional yeast, and mushroom powder.
- Dice up okra mixture in food processor.
- Mix with egg, whole wheat flour, and milk.
- Cook on fry pan in olive oil in a thin sheet.
- Flip when the batter is mostly solidified.
