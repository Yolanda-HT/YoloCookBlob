# Pumpkin Cake
> Category: Baby Food - Carb

## 1. Ingredients

### 1.1 Base
- Whole wheat flour: 90 g
- Glutinous rice flour: 30 g
- Baking powder: 1 tsp
- Egg yolk: 3
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp

### 1.2 Pumpkin Version
- Pumpkin: 1 can (425g)
- Mozzarella: 60g
- Milk: 120g

### 1.3 Chickpea Version
- Chickpea: 300 g (1 can)
- Cheddar cheese (finely shredded): 60 g
- Milk: 240 ml
- Nutritional yeast: 1/2 tbsp

## 2. Steps
- Mix base ingredients with either the pumpkin version ingredients or the chickpea version ingredients.
- Spray glass container with oil.
- Pour mixture into prepared glass container.
- Steam for 20 to 30 minutes, until center is set.
