# Iron Grapenuts Banana Oat Muffins
> Category: Baby Food - Carb <br>
> Yield: 12 muffins

## 1. Ingredients

### 1.1 To Blend
- Grapenuts cereal: 1 cup
- Sunflower seeds: 1/2 cup heaping
- Formula: 2 cups
- Vegetable oil: 1/4 cup
- Egg: 1
- Vanilla: 1 tbsp

### 1.2 To Mash and Mix
- Earth's Best multigrain infant cereal: 1 cup
- Ground flax seeds: 1/2 cup
- Chia seeds: 1/4 cup
- Ground cinnamon: 1 tsp
- Mashed banana: 1 cup (about 3 large bananas)
- Tahini: 2 tbsp
- Baking powder: 1 tbsp

## 2. Steps
- Preheat oven to 350F.
- Line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners or grease with fat.
- Process blend mix in food processor.
- Mash banana and mix with infant cereal, ground flax seeds, chia seeds, cinnamon, tahini, and baking powder.
- Gradually add blended mixture to the banana mixture. Stir just until combined. Do not overmix.
- Divide batter evenly into muffin cups.
- Bake for 30 to 40 minutes, or until set. Do not over-bake or the muffins will be dry.
