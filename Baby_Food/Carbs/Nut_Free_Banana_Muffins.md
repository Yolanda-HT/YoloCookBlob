# Nut Free Banana Muffins
> Category: Baby Food - Carb <br>
> Yield: 9 muffins

## 1. Ingredients

### 1.1 To Mix
- Ripe bananas (mashed): 3 large
- Creamy SunButter: 1/2 cup
- Eggs: 2 (or flax eggs: 2)
- Greek yogurt: 1/2 cup
- Vanilla extract: 2 tsp

### 1.2 Dry Ingredients
- Protein oats: 1/2 cup heaping, ground in food processor
- Flour: 1/2 cup
- Flax seeds: 1/4 cup
- Baking powder: 1 tbsp
- Cinnamon: 1/2 tsp

### 1.3 Optional Add-ins
- Blueberries

## 2. Steps
- Preheat oven to 350F. Line a muffin tray with 9 liners.
- In a large bowl, whisk mashed banana, SunButter, eggs, Greek yogurt, and vanilla until smooth.
- Stir in ground oats, flour, flax seeds, baking powder, and cinnamon until fully combined.
- Add optional mix-ins if desired.
- Spoon batter into muffin cups, filling about 90% full.
- Bake at 350F for about 15 minutes.
- Let cool slightly, then enjoy.
