# Spongy Cubes
> Category: Baby Food - Carb

## 1. Ingredients

### 1.1 Dry Ingredients
- Whole wheat flour: 40 g
- Glutinous rice flour: 20 g
- Baking powder: 1/4 tsp

### 1.2 To Fold
- Egg white: 2 (whipped to soft peaks)
- Lemon juice: 1/2 tsp

### 1.3 Chickpea Version
- Egg yolk: 2
- Chickpea: 60 g
- Cheddar cheese (finely shredded): 20 g
- Milk: 30 ml

### 1.4 Spinach Version
- Egg yolk: 2
- Squeezed spinach: 40g
- Cheddar cheese: 20g
- Milk: 30 ml

### 1.5 Apple Version
- Egg yolk: 1
- Apple: 60g
- Greek yogurt: 30g

## 2. Steps
- Preheat steamer to high heat.
- Mix dry ingredients in a bowl.
- Blend egg yolk and the remaining version ingredients in food processor.
- Combine dry mixture into wet mixture to form a thick batter.
- Whip egg white with lemon juice to soft peaks.
- Gently fold egg white into batter.
- Pour into molds, about 2 to 3 cm thick.
- Steam on high heat for 20 minutes.
- Cool slightly before removing and cutting into pieces.

## Notes
- Texture: soft, moist, slightly springy.
- For softer texture: add more milk.
- If sticky: reduce glutinous rice flour slightly next batch.
