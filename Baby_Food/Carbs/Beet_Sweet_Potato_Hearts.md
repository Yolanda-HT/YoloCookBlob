# Beet Sweet Potato Hearts
> Category: Baby Food - Carb <br>
> Yield: 36 hearts

## 1. Ingredients

### 1.1 To Blend
- Egg: 1 large
- Plain Greek yogurt: 1/4 cup
- Cooked beets (peeled): 8.8 oz
- Mashed sweet potato: 1/2 cup
- Neutral oil: 2 tbsp
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp

### 1.2 Dry Ingredients
- Whole wheat flour: 1/2 cup
- Ground flaxseed: 2 tbsp
- Nutritional yeast: 1 tbsp
- Baking powder: 1 tsp

## 2. Steps
- Preheat oven to 350F.
- Line or lightly grease the pan.
- Blend egg, Greek yogurt, vanilla, beets, sweet potato, and oil until mostly smooth.
- In a separate bowl, mix whole wheat flour, flaxseed, nutritional yeast, and baking powder.
- Stir wet mixture into dry ingredients until just combined.
- Pipe batter into pan.
- Bake until center is set. Little hearts take about 12 minutes.
