# Beef Potato Leek Patties
> Category: Baby Food - Protein <br>
> Yield: 9 patties

## 1. Ingredients
- Beef potato stew: 1.5 cup
- Nutritional yeast sauteed leek: 1
- Egg: 2
- Corn starch: 2 tbsp

## 2. Steps
- Mix beef potato stew, sauteed leek, eggs, and corn starch until evenly combined.
- Spray olive oil on Bruno small pancake plate.
- Spoon or pipe mixture into the small pancake plate.
- Cook on Bruno small pancake plate until patties are completely set. Makes 9 patties.
