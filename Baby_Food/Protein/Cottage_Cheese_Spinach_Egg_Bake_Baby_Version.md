# Cottage Cheese Spinach & Egg Bake (Baby Version)
> Category: Baby Food - Protein <br>
> Yield: 12 muffin size servings

## 1. Ingredients

### 1.1 To Saute and Dice Up
- Olive oil: 1 tsp
- Small onion (chopped): 1 (about 1 cup)
- Cremini mushrooms (sliced): 8 oz
- Baby spinach (roughly chopped): 5 cups (150g)

### 1.2 To Whisk
- Eggs: 9
- Low-fat cottage cheese: 1 1/2 cups
- Garlic powder: 1 tsp
- Onion powder: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/4 tsp
- Sliced cheddar: 1 slice
- Whole wheat flour: 1/4 cup

## 2. Steps
- Preheat oven to 350F.
- Lightly spray a muffin pan with nonstick spray.
- Heat olive oil in a large non-stick saute pan.
- Add onion and mushrooms. Cook 6 to 8 minutes until onions are translucent and mushrooms are browned.
- Add chopped spinach and cook briefly until wilted. Remove from heat.
- Dice up sauteed vegetables.
- In a large bowl, whisk together eggs, cottage cheese, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, cheddar, and whole wheat flour.
- Stir in sauteed vegetables.
- Pour mixture into prepared muffin pan and spread evenly.
- Bake for 25 minutes, or until center is set and top is lightly golden.
- Cool slightly before serving. Serve warm or refrigerate for meal prep.
