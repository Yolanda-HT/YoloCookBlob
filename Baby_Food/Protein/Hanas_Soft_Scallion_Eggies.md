# Hana's Soft Scallion Eggies
> Category: Baby Food - Protein

## 1. Ingredients
- Egg: 1
- Water: 1/2 cup
- Nutritional yeast: 1/2 tsp
- Scallion: 1

## 2. Steps
- Mix everything with a fork.
- Microwave with power 3 out of 10, until egg is completely set.
  - 1 egg: 4 to 6 minutes
  - 2 eggs: 8 to 12 minutes
