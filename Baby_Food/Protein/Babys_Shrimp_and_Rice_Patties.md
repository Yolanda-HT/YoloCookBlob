# Baby's Shrimp & Rice Patties
> Category: Baby Food - Protein

## 1. Ingredients

### 1.1 Base
- Rice: 2 cups
- Chicken broth: 1/2 cup
- Chopped scallions: 4

### 1.2 Shrimp Version
- Raw seasoned shrimps: 1 lb
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Italian seasoning: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tsp
- Egg: 1

### 1.3 Chicken Version
- Pulled chicken: 10 oz
- Eggs: 2
- Mochiko sweet rice flour: 2 tbsp

## 2. Steps
- Thaw shrimp and pat dry.
- Season shrimp with olive oil, black pepper, Italian seasoning, and lemon juice.
- Blend shrimp mix in food processor.
- Mix rice, chicken broth, chopped scallions, egg, and shrimp mix until evenly combined.
- For chicken version, mix rice, chicken broth, chopped scallions, pulled chicken, eggs, and Mochiko sweet rice flour until evenly combined.
- Cook on Bruno hot plate with 6-grid plate.
- Flip once the top starts to solidify.
