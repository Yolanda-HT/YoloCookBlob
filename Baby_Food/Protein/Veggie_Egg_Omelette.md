# Veggie Egg Omelette
> Category: Baby Food - Protein <br>
> Yield: 4 pancakes

## 1. Ingredients
- Diced sauteed vegetable: 1/2 cup
- Eggs: 3
- Bread: 1 slice
- Starch: 1 tbsp
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Nutritional yeast: 1/3 tbsp
- Milk: 2 tbsp

## 2. Steps
- Spray olive oil on Bruno 6 multi plate.
- Cook mixture on Bruno 6 multi plate. Makes 4 pancakes.
