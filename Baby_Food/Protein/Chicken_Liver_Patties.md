# Chicken Liver Patties
> Category: Baby Food - Protein <br>
> Yield: 12 patties

## 1. Ingredients

### 1.1 To Stir Fry
- Chicken liver: 1 can (18 oz)
- Leek: 1
- Ginger powder: 1/2 tsp
- Onion powder: 1/2 tsp
- Garlic powder: 1/2 tsp
- Mushroom powder: 1/2 tbsp
- Nutritional yeast: 1/2 tbsp

### 1.2 To Mix
- Cooked potato: 12 oz
- Diced scallions: 8
- Milk: 1/2 cup
- Whole wheat flour: 1/4 cup

## 2. Steps
- Stir fry chicken liver, leek, ginger powder, onion powder, garlic powder, mushroom powder, and nutritional yeast until fragrant and evenly combined.
- Mash cooked potato.
- Mix stir-fried chicken liver mixture with mashed potato, diced scallions, milk, and whole wheat flour until evenly combined.
- Spray olive oil on Bruno small pancake plate.
- Spoon or pipe mixture into the small pancake plate.
- Cook on Bruno small pancake plate until patties are completely set. Makes 12 patties.
