# Maple Blondies  
> Origin 08/18/2025 <br>  
> Yield: 1 pan (9×13 inch), 24 servings

## 1. Ingredients
### 1.1 To brown and mix
- Salted butter: 1.5 sticks  
- Dark brown sugar: 3/4 cup  
- Maple syrup: 1/4 cup  
- Vegetable oil: 1/4 cup  
- Apple butter: 2 tbsp  
- Egg: 1  
- Vanilla extract: 1 tbsp  
- Maple extract: 1.5 tbsp  

### 1.2 Dry ingredients
- All-purpose flour: 2 1/4 cups  
- Baking powder: 1 tsp  
- Ground cinnamon: 1/2 tsp  
- Kosher salt: 1/2 tsp  

### 1.3 To fold in
- Semi-sweet chocolate chips: 2/3 cup  
- Pecan halves: 1 cup  

### 1.4 Glaze
- Salted butter: 3 tbsp  
- Maple syrup: 3 tbsp  
- Powdered sugar: 3/4 cup  
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp 
- Maple extract: 2 tsps

## 2. Steps
### 2.1 Prep and browning
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 9×13 inch baking dish with parchment paper.  
- In a skillet, brown 1.5 sticks of butter over medium heat until toasted (~2–3 min).  
- Transfer to a mixing bowl and stir in the remaining 0.5 stick butter. Let cool 5 minutes.  

### 2.2 Mix wet and dry
- Add brown sugar, maple syrup, apple butter, egg, vanilla extract, and maple extract to browned butter. Beat until creamy.  
- Mix in flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt.  

### 2.3 Fold and bake
- Fold in chocolate chips and pecan halves.  
- Spread dough evenly into prepared baking dish.  
- Bake at 350°F for 18–22 minutes, until edges are set and center is soft.  
- Let cool completely.  

### 2.4 Glaze
- Melt butter and maple syrup in a small pot.  
- Remove from heat and whisk in powdered sugar, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt.  
- Drizzle glaze over cooled bars.  
- Let glaze set for 5 minutes before cutting into squares.

## Note:
- Store at room temp or in fridge for 3–4 days.  
- For quicker cooling, freeze bars for 15 minutes before glazing.
